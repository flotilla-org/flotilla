## Artifacts

Use `flotilla artifact put --kind <kind> --about <subject> --summary key=value <path>` for durable crew work. The daemon reads the file, so pass a path rather than its contents. Prefer Markdown for prose; self-contained HTML is also accepted. Use `--about` for the exact subject (such as a commit or review round), and `--summary` for small scalar facts that workflow conditions can query, not a duplicate of the body.

- `explainer`: human-readable explanation of a completed change.
- `decision-ledger`: decisions made while carrying out the work.
- `review-round`: one review verdict and its evidence.
- `brief`: instructions or context handed to another crew.
- `review-bundle`: durable review evidence collected across rounds.
- `recording`, `test-report`, and `raw-test-output`: bulky supporting material with short retention; keep conclusions in prose artifacts.
